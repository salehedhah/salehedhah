# Motion Graphic Style: "Brand Story" Reel

Reference clip: [`reference.mp4`](./reference.mp4) (Starbucks brand-story example).
Sample frames: [`frames/`](./frames).

Saved so this style can be reused as a template for future Remotion videos
(e.g. with the `remotion-create` / `remotion-markup` skills).

## Format

- Vertical, 720x1280 (9:16), 30fps, ~12s.
- Built for Reels/TikTok/Shorts.

## Background

- Flat, muted sage/off-white background (roughly `#E7EBE6`).
- A large, softly blurred, low-opacity watermark of the brand logo sits
  centered behind everything, giving depth without competing with the text.
- Slowly rotating, heavily-blurred black "petal"/leaf abstract shapes drift
  in the corners as ambient motion (parallax filler, never in focus).
- A diagonal brand-green ribbon/stripe occasionally sweeps behind the photo
  card, entering/exiting frame at an angle rather than straight edges.

## Typography

- Two-weight mixed type on every caption line:
  - A light, italic sans-serif for connective words ("This is how", "It was", "More than").
  - A bold, italic **script/cursive** for the emphasized word or brand name
    (larger, heavier, often the visual focal point of the line).
- Text is dark charcoal/black, centered or left-aligned depending on layout,
  and kept short — one clause per beat.
- Lines fade/slide in softly (no hard cuts on text).

## Imagery

- Product shots (e.g. the cup) are flat-lay / isolated on the background,
  sometimes ringed by a thin black **dashed circle** guide for emphasis.
- Story/context photos are placed in a **rounded-corner card** with a soft
  drop shadow, floating above the background — never full-bleed.
- Cards slide/scale in per beat, held ~1.5–2s, then transition to the next.

## Motion & Pacing

- Smooth cross-fades and gentle slide/scale-ins, no jump cuts.
- Ambient background elements (logo watermark, blurred petals, ribbon) move
  continuously and slowly, independent of the foreground beat changes —
  this keeps the frame alive even on static caption cards.
- Each "beat" = one short line of copy + one image, ~1–2s, chained into a
  short narrative arc (hook → context → payoff).

## Palette

- Background: muted sage `#E7EBE6`-ish.
- Accent: brand green (Starbucks green `#00704A` / dark green `#1E3932`).
- Text: near-black charcoal.
- Photography stays naturalistic (warm interior tones, cool blue night
  tones) and pops against the desaturated background.

## Reuse notes

When asked to build a video "in this style", replicate:
1. Sage background + centered blurred brand-logo watermark.
2. Mixed italic-sans + bold-script caption typography, 2 weights per line.
3. Rounded-corner photo cards with drop shadow for context shots; dashed
   circle for product hero shots.
4. Slow ambient background motion (blurred petal shapes, diagonal ribbon)
   independent of the foreground text/image beats.
5. Short beats (~1.5–2s) with soft cross-fade/slide transitions.
